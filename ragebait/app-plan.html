The Rage Baiter Project Plan

Austin Ashcraft

WDD 131

Overview

Purpose

Create a fun and slightly frustrating game to test people's limits.

Audience

Students and other people who want to waste time for just a few minutes and do something out of the ordinary.

Dynamic elements

There will be multiple objects and the user needs to click and drag an image into the right object. There will also be a dynamic image that grows in size every time the user clicks and drags something onto it until it hits a certain size. An image will need to be clicked on in just the right spot to pass a level. On one page the mouse will move the wrong way, and the button will be unclickable with the mouse, but can be clicked through tab and enter.

Pseudocode for your project

The site will load and display the first level. The user will need to click the word start to display the next js object. The next object will have manipulated mouse movements and the button will be unclickable, but by keyboard they can get to the next object. Two images will be displayed, and they will be the same image with a claim that they are different. One image will have a small spot on it where if they click there the next object will be displayed. There will be multiple 'cradles' displayed and the user must click and drag a cat onto the correct cradle object, which will be random. There will be a caterpillar that is displayed in the next object with a basket of apples that must be dragged to the caterpillar to make it grow. The caterpillar will need 25 apples to make it grow enough where it 'pushes' a button. The final page/object will be displayed at the end.
